Housing Strategy

Cornwall's housing crisis demands action. Local people are being priced out while second homes and holiday lets hollow out communities. We champion restrictions on second-home conversions, genuinely affordable housing on public land, and local connection requirements that work.

  • check_circle End the housing crisis for Cornish families

{{/* Environment — Vertical Dark Card */}}

Our Environment

Cornwall's coastline, moorland, rivers, and hedgerows are assets and responsibilities. Any green transition must come with practical, fully costed support — not just targets.

{{/* Cost of Living */}}

Cost of Living

Low wages and high costs — Cornwall's defining economic squeeze. We press for a fairer funding settlement, local procurement, and support for community energy schemes.

{{/* Jobs & Economy */}}

Jobs & Economy

Sustainable growth that benefits Cornish people. Stronger partnerships between the council, colleges, and employers so young people can build careers here.

{{/* Health & Care */}}

Health & Social Care

Adequate funding for adult social care, improved mental health services, and rural access to healthcare. Cornwall's recruitment crisis needs targeted, funded solutions.

{{/* Secondary Local Grid */}}

Part Two: Cornwall's Voice at Westminster

Cornwall's interests are not being served by national parties. A CING MP would go to Parliament with one job: to fight for Cornwall.

{{/* A Fair Deal for Cornwall */}}

A Fair Deal for Cornwall

Cornwall consistently receives less per head in government funding than comparable areas. Funding formulas designed around urban centres systematically disadvantage rural, peripheral areas. A CING MP would make the case for fairness at every opportunity.

"We seek sustainable economic growth and improved job opportunities for Cornish people — backed by a funding formula that recognises rurality, deprivation, and distance."

{{/* Fishing, Farming & Food Security */}}

Fishing, Farming & Food Security

Post-Brexit promises to fishing and farming communities have not been kept. Cornwall's primary industries need practical support, fairer trade deals, and less red tape. Food security is national security — domestic producers deserve better.

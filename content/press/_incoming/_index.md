This folder is a staging area. Drop raw `.md`, `.docx`, or `.txt` drafts here. Claude (or a human editor) will convert them into the final format and move them into the appropriate meeting sub-folder (e.g. `../full-council-2026-04-21/`).

The `_build` cascade above prevents everything in this folder and its descendants from being rendered, listed, or having its resources published.
